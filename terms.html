Terms of Use

Retainer · last updated 18 October 2026

Retainer is a journal for the things you do, and a way to share some of them with people you choose. These terms cover what you can share, how you are protected, and what to do if something goes wrong.

The short version. Only share what you are happy for the people on that action to see, and nothing illegal or harmful. If someone upsets you, leave the action or remove them. If something is illegal, tell the police.

Who these terms are from

Retainer is made by Tom Walton, contactable at retainerapp@icloud.com. Apple’s standard licence agreement for App Store apps applies to the app as well.

Who can use Retainer

Retainer is for adults: you need to be 18 or over to use it.

Your journal stays yours

  • What you write and the photos you add belong to you. Retainer claims no rights over them.
  • They are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and I cannot see them. The privacy policy explains how.
  • On a shared action, everyone on that action can see your check-ins: notes, photos and any place you attach. Only share what you are happy for them to see.

What you must not share

On an action you share with anyone, do not post:

  • anything illegal, including sexual images of children, terrorist material, or anything that helps someone commit a crime;
  • threats, harassment or bullying, or abuse aimed at someone because of who they are;
  • intimate or sexual images of anyone without their consent, including fakes, or sexual images sent to someone who did not ask for them;
  • pornography;
  • anything that encourages suicide, self-harm or an eating disorder, or explains how;
  • violent or gory images meant to shock, or anything encouraging violence;
  • dangerous challenges or stunts, or anything selling or promoting drugs, weapons or taking harmful substances;
  • anything mocking or shaming someone’s body or appearance;
  • anything that urges others towards hopelessness or despair;
  • scams, or someone else’s private details or photos without their permission.

Writing honestly about how you feel, including when things are hard, is what a journal is for, and is always welcome.

How you are protected

  • Only people you invite can see an action you share. There are no public profiles, no search for people, and no way for a stranger to reach you through Retainer.
  • You can leave any action someone has shared with you, at any time. It disappears from your iPhone.
  • The owner of an action can stop sharing it, remove anyone from it, and delete any check-in on it.

What I can and cannot do

Everything shared on Retainer lives in the iCloud accounts of the people on that action. I cannot see it, scan it or remove it, and that is deliberate: it is what keeps your journal private, including from me. So if something goes wrong on an action, the people who can act on it are the people on that action, and the police.

If something goes wrong

If someone is in danger right now, call 999, or your local emergency number outside the UK.

If you think something is illegal, report it to the police, and do this before anything is deleted, so they can see it. In the UK you can call 101, or report online to your local police force. You can also report:

  • sexual images of children, or someone grooming a child, to CEOP, the National Crime Agency’s child protection service;
  • terrorist material at gov.uk/report-terrorism.

If something on an action upsets you, press and hold the check-in and choose Not comfortable with this check-in? If it is your action, you can delete the check-in or remove the person who posted it. Otherwise, you can leave the action, and it disappears from your iPhone. Nobody is told that you did this.

If you are under 18 and something has upset you, you can talk to Childline for free on 0800 1111.

How illegal content is dealt with

Retainer does not use any technology to scan, filter or detect content, and I cannot see or remove what is shared. So, for terrorist material, child sexual abuse material and any other illegal content alike, the fastest way to get it off an action is for the action’s owner to delete it. That removes it from everyone’s iPhone and from iCloud at once. Anyone else on the action can leave it, so it is gone from their iPhone straight away. And report it to the police first, as above, so they can see it.

Telling me about a problem with Retainer

If something is wrong with Retainer itself, or you want to complain about the app, email retainerapp@icloud.com. That includes complaints that I am not keeping to these terms, to the UK’s online safety rules, or to your rights to privacy and free expression.

Email is not the way to get help in an emergency or report a crime. I am one person, not a safety service, and I cannot see what is shared. Please use the routes above. And please do not send me shared content (screenshots, photos or copied notes): describe the problem in words.

What happens next

  • I read messages at least once a week, and will reply to say I have yours. A fuller answer can take up to two weeks, and a little longer if I am away.
  • What can come of it: an explanation, a change to the app or to these terms, or, if the problem is something I have done, putting it right where I can. I will tell you what I decide.
  • I will not tell anyone else that you have been in touch, including anyone your message is about.
  • If you would rather not hear back after my first reply, just say so.
  • If you disagree with how I have handled it, reply and I will look at it again.
