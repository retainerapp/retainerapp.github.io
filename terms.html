Terms of Use

Retainer · last updated 29 October 2026

Retainer is a journal for the things you do, and a way to share some of them with people you choose. These terms cover what you can share, how you are protected, and what to do if something goes wrong.

The short version. Only share what you are happy for the people on that action to see, and nothing illegal or harmful. If someone upsets you, leave the action or remove them. If something is illegal, tell the police.

Who these terms are from

Retainer is made by Tom Walton, contactable at retainerapp@icloud.com. Apple’s standard licence agreement for App Store apps applies to the app as well.

Who can use Retainer

Retainer is for adults: you need to be 18 or over to use it. Where the law requires it, Retainer checks this with Apple.

Your journal stays yours

  • What you write and the photos you add belong to you. Retainer claims no rights over them.
  • They are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and I cannot see them. The privacy policy explains how.
  • On a shared action, everyone on that action can see your check-ins: notes, photos and any place you attach. Only share what you are happy for them to see.

What you must not share

On an action you share with anyone, do not post:

  • anything illegal, including sexual images of children, terrorist material, or anything that helps someone commit a crime;
  • threats, harassment or bullying, or abuse aimed at someone because of who they are;
  • intimate or sexual images of anyone without their consent, including fakes, or sexual images sent to someone who did not ask for them;
  • pornography;
  • anything that encourages suicide, self-harm or an eating disorder, or explains how;
  • violent or gory images meant to shock, or anything encouraging violence;
  • dangerous challenges or stunts, or anything selling or promoting drugs, weapons or taking harmful substances;
  • anything mocking or shaming someone’s body or appearance;
  • anything that urges others towards hopelessness or despair;
  • scams, or someone else’s private details or photos without their permission.

Writing honestly about how you feel, including when things are hard, is what a journal is for, and is always welcome.

How you are protected

  • Only people you invite can see an action you share. There are no public profiles, no search for people, and no way for a stranger to reach you through Retainer.
  • You can leave any action someone has shared with you, at any time. It disappears from your iPhone.
  • The owner of an action can stop sharing it, remove anyone from it, and delete any check-in on it.

What I can and cannot do

Everything shared on Retainer lives in the iCloud accounts of the people on that action, not on any server of mine. So I cannot see it, and I cannot use any technology to scan, filter or remove it. That is also what keeps your journal private, including from me.

If any content of serious concern is shared, the people who can act on it are the people on that action, and the police. For terrorist material, child sexual abuse material and any other illegal content alike, you should always contact the police for advice in the first instance. If it needs removing, the action’s owner can delete it, which removes it from everyone’s iPhone and from iCloud at once. Anyone else on the action can leave it, so it is gone from their iPhone straight away.

If you’re not comfortable with something someone has shared

If someone is in immediate danger, get help straight away. If you think something is illegal, tell the police.

If something on an action upsets you, you can press and hold the check-in and choose Not comfortable with this check-in? If it is your action, you can delete the check-in or remove the person who posted it. Otherwise, you can leave the action, and it disappears from your iPhone. Retainer does not send anyone a message about it, but the others on the action will see that you have left.

Telling me about a problem with Retainer

If something is wrong with Retainer itself, or you want to complain about the app or how I run it, email retainerapp@icloud.com.

Email is not the way to get help in an emergency or report a crime. I am one person, not a safety service, and I cannot see what is shared. Please use the routes above. And please do not send me shared content (screenshots, photos or copied notes).

What happens next

  • I read messages at least once a week and will aim to get back to you within two weeks, or a little longer if I am away.
  • What can come of it: an explanation, a change to the app or to these terms, or, if the problem is something I have done, putting it right where I can. I will tell you what I decide.
  • I will not tell anyone else that you have been in touch, including anyone your message is about, unless the law requires me to.
  • If you disagree with how I have handled it, reply and I will look at it again.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, I will update this page and the date at the top.
