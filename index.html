Journal, track, share, repeat.

When did you last change the sheets? Call home? Go somewhere new together? Retainer remembers, with a note, a photo, a place, whatever you want to keep. Keep it to yourself or share it with the people closest to you. For those who’d rather keep track than keep score.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone

How long has it been?

Every card shows the answer, so one look tells you where things stand. Want a nudge? Retainer can remind you when it’s time. There are no streaks to break. Miss a month and nothing is lost.

Check in together

Invite your partner or anyone close to an action, so you can both check in with your own notes and photos. Share the good times: date nights, days out, the pubs you’ve tried. Share the jobs too: see when the cat last had its flea treatment and who did it, so nothing is missed or done twice. Everything else stays yours alone.

A journal that writes itself

Keeping a diary is hard. Retainer never hands you a blank page. Each check-in is a small entry, and month by month they become a record of your life that you never had to sit down and write. What the optician said. Which film you watched. How the garden looked this time last year.

A note, a photo and the place

Checking in takes a moment: add a few words, a photo and where you were, or nothing at all. Every place goes on a map, so you can find your way back to that beach, that walk, that pub. Forgot on the day? Add a photo you took and Retainer can set the date, time and place from it.

Private by design

No account, no ads. Your journal lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

  • Only the people you invite can see what you share
  • Works offline, and catches up when you’re back online
  • Your location is saved only if you allow it, and shown to others only if you choose
