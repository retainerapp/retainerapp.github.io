When did you last…?

When did you last phone Mum? Who gave the cat its flea treatment? Which wine did you love on holiday? Retainer helps you remember the things you’ve done, whatever they happen to be. Check in when something happens, see how long it’s been, and share individual activities with the people you choose.

Free on iPhone

How long has it been?

Every card shows the answer, so one look tells you where things stand. There are no streaks or scores to chase. Want a nudge? Add an optional reminder for when it’s time.

One place for all sorts of things

Changing the bedsheets, watering the garden, looking after your bike, finishing books, trying wines, visiting new places. Some things you do again and again. Others you simply want to remember. You shouldn’t need a different app just to keep a simple record of each one. Start with your own, or pick from more than a hundred ideas.

Who did it last?

Share an individual activity with your partner, a family member or a friend. You can both check in, add notes and photos, and see who did what and when. No more wondering who last watered the plants or gave the cat its flea treatment. And sharing one activity doesn’t mean sharing the rest.

A history worth keeping

Every check-in adds to a history you can look back on. It can be just a date, or you can include a note, photo or place. Remember what you thought of a book, which wine you enjoyed, where you went together, or how the garden looked last spring.

Remember where

Browse previous check-ins, look back through the calendar and statistics, or revisit saved places on a map. Forgot to check in at the time? Add an earlier date. Retainer can also use the date and location saved in a photo to help fill in the details.

Private by design

Your records live on your iPhone and sync through iCloud. No separate Retainer account, no ads, and no personal data collected by the developer.

  • Only the people you invite can see what you share
  • Works offline, and catches up when you’re back online
  • Your location is saved only if you allow it, and shown to others only if you choose
