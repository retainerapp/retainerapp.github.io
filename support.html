Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Retainer has no accounts, no sign-up and no password. It uses the iCloud account already on your iPhone, which is also why your data syncs to your other devices without you doing anything.

How do I share an action with someone?

Open the action, go to its Share tab, and send an invitation. It goes as a link through Messages, Mail or whatever you choose. They tap it, and the action appears in their copy of Retainer.

Can they see my other actions?

No — only the one you shared. That restriction is enforced by Apple’s servers rather than by the app, so it holds even if something in Retainer misbehaves.

Someone accepted my invitation but their name isn’t showing

Their name and photo arrive separately from the invitation itself, and can take a moment. Pull down to refresh on the actions list. If it still hasn’t appeared after a minute or two, ask them to open the app once — that is usually all it takes.

How do reminders work?

Two kinds. A recurring reminder fires at a time you pick, daily or weekly. An after check-in reminder fires a set period after the last time you checked in — which is the one you want for anything on a cycle, like a filter that needs replacing every three months.

After check-in reminders move every time you check in, and disappear when there are no check-ins left to count from.

Can I add a photo to a check-in?

Yes, and a note, and optionally the place you were. All three are optional — a check-in with nothing attached is perfectly normal, and is two taps.

If you attach a location to a check-in on a shared action, the people you share it with can see that location. If you would rather they did not, leave it off.

How do I delete everything?

Deleting the app removes the copy on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Retainer, and delete the data there. There is no copy of it anywhere else — see the privacy policy.

Is there an Android or iPad version?

Retainer is an iPhone app. There is no Android version and no plan for one.

Found a bug?

Please tell me. Email retainerapp@icloud.com with what you did and what happened instead. Screenshots help enormously, and a rough time of day lets me line it up with Apple’s crash reports.
